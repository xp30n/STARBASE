# DESCRIPTION

Our project is called STARBASE, a fictional space company that investigates and observes stars. Our idea for this project was to have a way to collect data and showcase it in an unconventional way through a game.
As a new employee, your job is done all in the office. At the end of every day, you are given data from the astronauts, and you have to figure out which star systems they went to. You are given the distance in light years from earth, the magnitude of their brightness and which constellation they are part of. Using the information you’re given, you need to go through the STARDEX to find which stars match the information that the astronaut gives you. 
Our project had a few inspirations. The visual and aesthetic inspiration comes from the game Lethal Company, where we wanted to create a terminal like the one in the game:

Another point of inspiration came from the game Krillion, where you are asked questions and have to give answers. The more niche your answer is, the better. Obviously we did not take the same mechanic, but the question and answer format is what we used for our game. 

We also talked through a lot of different iterations of the game. At first we created a whole story behind STARBASE to give it a little more life, but with time constraints and for the sake of simplicity, we just went with a terminal program.
