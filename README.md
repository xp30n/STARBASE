# WELCOME ABOARD. YOUR MISSION BEGINS HERE

## MISSION STATUS
You are now an ELITE employee of STARBASE, a space company devoted to the investigation and the observation of stars. 

Your job is to decipher where our atronauts went on their expeditions, and send that data back to STARBASE HQ. 

The catch is that not all of the data presented to you is available! 

Using your STARDEX, filter through the available star info and correctly input the star into your assignments page.

Thank you for your continued patronage.
